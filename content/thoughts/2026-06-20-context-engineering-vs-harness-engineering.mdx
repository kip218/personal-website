As AI progresses - I need to make sure I understand new concepts and phrases as they come up.

Something I've been hearing recently is harness engineering - and it seems to be an evolution of context engineering. Both dealing with how to better use AI to fit your specific need.

When "prompt engineering" was becoming a thing - I always thought it was a little gimmicky. Sure, writing good prompts is important, but prompt "engineering"? That felt like a stretch.

Using Claude in my daily workflow, I quickly realized that there definitely is a standard set of best practices that can help you maximize the efficacy of AI to fit your specific use case. This was when I discovered context engineering
