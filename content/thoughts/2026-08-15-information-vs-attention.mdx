I recently watched a Hank Green video where he talked about how in recent years, AI has accelerated the rate at which information is generated online, and thus *attention* has become a scarcer resource than the wealth of information that exists out there. In short, there is way more content online than there are people to consume it, and this will only become truer as time goes on.

For me - currently I'm juggling a full time job, a full time masters in data science, and also balancing my personal projects, which has actually been taking up most of my mind recently. I can only really juggle so much of this in my head because of AI. Claude allows me to get assignments done faster, tasks organized more effectively, and delegate work where possible. For many of my personal projects, I'm able to focus on the high level design and architecture and spend less time on the low level debugging.

That also got me thinking, that while yes, AI is helping me juggle a lot of things and now I am able to do more with less time, the real bottleneck is my mental capacity, my ability to actually spend time with difficult problems, study meaningful concepts, and internalize them. Claude can help summarize things and generate a lot of output, but I still need to read it and spend time with it to internalize it. In the end, as Hank says in his video, as information becomes abundant, my time and attention becomes the scarcer resource and the bottleneck.
