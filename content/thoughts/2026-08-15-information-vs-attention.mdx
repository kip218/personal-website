I recently watched a Hank Green video where he talked about how in recent years, especially with AI, wealth of information is scarcer than attention. He referenced a person that coined this phrase in 1970s or so, which is really early for the times.

It's got me thinking how true that is - currently I'm juggling a full time job, a full time masters in data science, and also balancing my personal projects, which has actually been taking up most of my mind recently (mobile app dev with my girlfriend).

The thing is, I can only really juggle so much of this in my head because of AI. Claude allows me to take certain shortcuts and get assignments done faster, tasks organized more effectively, and delegate work.

That also got me thinking though, that while yes, AI is helping me juggle a lot of things and now I am able to do more with less time, the real bottleneck is my mental capacity, my ability to actually spend time with difficult problems, study meaningful concepts, and internalize them.
