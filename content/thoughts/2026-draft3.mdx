Everything is AI now.

Internal tools, messages and documentation from coworkers, triage messages and next steps.

Everything is just AI generated, reviewed by someone using AI, feedback document provided by AI, for it to be summarized and addressed with AI.

At a higher level, to the people further from the typical day-to-day IC work, it sounds like work gets done faster, and people are working more efficiently. But in this process of AI regurgitating AI content, there is something missing that builds up - something that humans have been doing in the old workflow cycle.

I cannot express how annoying and stressful it is to investigate an issue for hours, and finally raise it as an internal question, for someone to just give an AI explanation.
