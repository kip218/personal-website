Recently I was talking about the Hugging Face AI incident and the security breach that happened. Again as I said previously, I'm a pretty skeptical person. When I first heard about it (not just about this particular incident but whenever I hear about an incident that is related to AI escaping the sandbox, an AI plotting to take over the world, or something like that), I become very, very skeptical. That is because I just don't believe that some of these sensational headlines and articles are written in good faith. They are deliberately picking and choosing words that would generate the most views, generate the most clicks, and one way to do that is to exaggerate and dramatize everything as much as possible.
